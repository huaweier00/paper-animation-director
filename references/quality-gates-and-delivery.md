# Quality gates and delivery

## S0–S3 — social editorial quality

Apply S0–S2 before P0 when the destination is Douyin or a comparable feed. Apply S3 after P3 and before the social file is released:

- **S0 contract** — `platform` and `social_contract` name one primary audience, an honest editorial promise, a familiarity gap for well-known material, a feed/aspect decision, an opening event and line, a three-second proof, a transferable insight, a concrete save object, a cover contract, and `premium-quality-first` posture.
- **S1 hook proof** — a rendered non-deliverable 0–3 second animatic begins with an event, anomaly, choice, result, or compelling relation; contains no pre-promise logo/series/source intro; works muted and with sound; and honestly matches the cover.
- **S2 full edit proof** — a complete scratch animatic proves every segment advances conflict, choice, consequence, emotion, insight, anticipation, or deliberate observation; familiar material has a new angle; the ending fulfils the opening; and the save object is useful and readable.
- **S3 feed proof** — the final render passes a vertical-feed simulation and the cover passes thumbnail review. Platform controls or metadata do not cover faces, critical contacts, captions, episode title, or save object.

Stop before formal voice, identity generation, or premium shot assets when S0–S2 fails. Return to script or edit, not polish. Stop social delivery when S3 fails.

## V0–V4 — visual direction before premium assets

Apply these gates after S2 for social work, or after the equivalent story edit for another `premium-quality-first` route. Read `visual-art-direction-and-asset-quality.md` for the full contract.

- **V0 audience and emotion** — declare the immediate read, adult/repeat-viewer reward, emotional promise, collectible-frame goal, and undesirable impression.
- **V1 art routes** — compare at least three materially different reference-only directions by design logic, strengths, risks, and likely audience impression.
- **V2 hero triad** — prove reference-only opening-pressure, central-choice/conflict, and consequence/save frames from approved scratch compositions; select one route with a written rationale.
- **V3 integration** — prove one representative character, critical prop, and environment share perspective, light, edge treatment, texture scale, atmosphere, support contact, and phone-size readability.
- **V4 style lock** — approve shape, value, color, line/texture, light/depth, composition, signature, anti-generic list, phone-size, grayscale, and blur/focal reviews.

Do not begin formal identity generation or shot assets while V0–V4 is incomplete. Look-development frames remain `reference_only: true` and never become production shortcuts.

## P0 — semantic clarity

- The story remains broadly understandable with audio muted.
- Every key verb has an observable proof frame or interval.
- Every narrated or plot-bearing noun is present, recognizable, and the correct object class.
- Actor, object, cause, and result are unambiguous.
- The visible action uses the semantic target named by narration and the shot contract; a window, doorway, or nearby prop cannot silently replace a required wall, seat, container, or other target.
- Weapons, plants, currency/treasure, official documents, vehicles, tools, and symbolic connectors use real construction, scale, material cues, support, and state changes rather than abstract lines, icons, piles, or placeholder marks.
- Shared work, refusal, rotation, rescue, and other relational actions are staged rather than merely narrated.
- A command that changes a group state shows the initial state, issuing gesture/line, propagated response, and readable final state.

Stop and restage when P0 fails.

Run a muted visual pass before adding polish. If a reviewer cannot identify actor, location, action, and result without narration, the scene is not ready for effects, music, or final typography.

## P1 — identity and physical continuity

- The rendered shot matches the approved art route, signature, and project-specific forbidden-trait list.
- One focal order survives normal phone size, grayscale, and blur/focal review; decorative detail does not overpower the face, action, or critical object.
- Value separation, palette role, key light, depth falloff, line hierarchy, and texture scale remain coherent with the visual-direction contract.
- Character, prop, and environment share perspective, light, edge treatment, material treatment, atmosphere, support contact, and believable occlusion rather than appearing pasted together.
- Every visually dominant approved asset has candidate comparison, rejection reasons, intended display scale, source dimensions, composite proof, phone-size proof, and finish approval.
- Character identity, anatomy, costume, and scale remain stable.
- The frontal identity image remains reference-only and never appears as a keyed, animated, or delivered shot layer.
- Heads and feet are complete; no chroma plate or rectangular residue remains.
- No frame edge, `overflow`, crop, alpha boundary, mask, matte, transition, foreground, subtitle, or watermark slices through the scalp, facial contour, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, or neck and produces a half-face or amputated-head effect.
- Natural profile preserves coherent skull and facial anatomy. Any partial head/face occlusion has a visible physical occluder, declared depth order/time window/reason, and an unobstructed identity-proof frame before or after it.
- Actor start/end zones, travel direction, facing, feet, torso, gaze, and entry/exit agree with the approved spatial contract.
- Named motion corridors remain clear and provide enough room for the actor, costume, carried load, contact, and reaction.
- Every standing person, animal, wheel, vehicle, rooted plant, hanging object, and placed prop remains attached to its named support surface or attachment target.
- Feet, hooves, wheels, seats, roots, knots, bowstrings, containers, and contact shadows share credible perspective, scale, and load.
- Hands, shoulders, ropes, poles, containers, and shared loads make contact.
- Water is inside containers; effects originate and land correctly.
- Prop ownership and retirement are deterministic.
- Foreground occlusion, rim masks, and contact shadows place subjects in one space.

## P2 — motion, voice, and pacing

- Pose phases and travel agree.
- Every required moving asset has current hash-bound asset facts, an implementation-audited motion contract, a compiled track consumed by the composition, and rendered proof at the named entry/contact/settle/exit times.
- Observed rendered facing and travel agree with the contract; support, contact, result, and mirror authorization are reviewed from MP4 frames rather than inferred from source art.
- No wrong-facing asset is mirrored, rotated, translated, or otherwise force-fit without a documented symmetry, handedness, text, light, contact, and continuity audit.
- No teleport, unexplained duplicate, frozen tail, or decorative-only dead zone remains.
- Narration uses approved natural playback and measured timing.
- Caption changes do not overlap unreadably.
- Scene completion is followed by a cut, earned reaction, or transition within roughly 0.6–1.2 seconds.
- Transitions clarify continuity, time, location, or energy instead of imitating slides.
- Voice-only review confirms speaker ownership, accent, emotional register, and absence of overlap.
- The expected-line ledger confirms that every required narration and dialogue stem is present at the locked time; music-only or BGM replacement renders must not silently remove speech.
- Captions default to centered safe placement and do not cover proof contacts or get clipped at the frame edge.
- Social shots deliver the declared `visual_beats`; a camera push, grain, dust, or texture loop does not pass as a new beat unless it materially changes meaning, attention, mood, reveal, or transition.
- Every social interval that exceeds the project’s visual-beat expectation has an approved `long_hold_rationale` and provides sustained performance, suspense, observation, sound, or reflection in the scratch and final edits.
- The opening event, first-three-second proof, consequence, and ending save object remain at their approved global times after final voice fitting.

## P3 — technical delivery

- HyperFrames strict check passes.
- Keyframe diagnostics and focused shots prove real action selectors.
- Midpoints, transitions, proof frames, first frame, final-minus-hold, and final frame are visually reviewed.
- Frames extracted from the rendered MP4 match the approved preview.
- Review first, midpoint, every pose change, intentional-occlusion entry/maximum/exit, proof, transition, and final frames. Enlarge each visible head and face enough to inspect scalp, facial contour, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, neck connection, and mask edges.
- Never treat a stale Studio tab, a local file preview, or one unrendered screenshot as delivery proof; refresh the project server and inspect rendered-MP4 frames.
- FFprobe confirms plausible duration, resolution, frame rate, and intended audio streams.
- A full decode completes without errors; the final mix is compared with the expected voice ledger or voice-only reference mix so an audio stream containing only music cannot pass.
- Moving watermark is visible on both light and dark rendered frames while avoiding captions and sustained face coverage.
- The archival master is preserved.
- The social H.264 derivative uses Fast Start and reaches the configured VMAF floor, normally 95 or higher.
- Hand-painted line work, subtitle edges, faces, and low-contrast mineral colors remain intact in beginning/middle/climax/ending frames extracted from the compressed social file.
- Thumbnail, normal phone-feed, and enlarged-detail reviews preserve focal hierarchy, face/action readability, line/texture finish, and the declared visual signature after compression.

## Per-shot release rule

Keep a limited WIP window of two or three adjacent, fully planned shots after the benchmark so continuity can be reviewed and asset/animation work can overlap. Release final shots one at a time and in sequence order. The current release candidate must have:

- an approved semantic and spatial contract;
- approved shot-specific assets and critical-prop recognizability specs;
- an approved visual-direction contract plus candidate/composite evidence for visually dominant assets;
- a rendered draft MP4;
- muted, voice-only, combined, and real-world plausibility reviews;
- first, midpoint, contact, proof, and final extracted frames;
- a written `approved` decision.

Use shot-release schema version 5 for new premium work. It must explicitly declare `motion_required` and bind its rendered MP4, every required upstream record, and every proof frame to current SHA-256 values using `scripts/bind_release_evidence.py`. Version 5 additionally binds the approved project medium contract, the shot performance contract, and the project audio contract; it requires `medium_truth`, `performance_contract`, `pose_reuse`, and `sound_action_sync` checks. All shots point to the approved visual-direction contract and pass art-direction match, focal hierarchy, value separation, palette/light coherence, character/environment integration, asset finish, and phone-size readability. A dynamic shot additionally points to the current motion contract and an approved hash-bound rendered-motion review, and passes `motion_integrity`. A static shot declares `motion_required: false`, supplies earned-stillness evidence in its performance contract, and does not fabricate motion.

After all ordered shot releases pass, bind `release-index.json` with `scripts/bind_release_index.py` and audit it with `scripts/audit_release_index.py`. The release index must exactly match story order, reject stale shot-release hashes, audit cross-shot pose reuse, verify the final master hash and decode, and verify the declared audio mode against the actual master stream.

Do not release the next shot while the current ordered decision is `rejected`, `needs-fix`, or undocumented. Do not open a new WIP window while any dependency shared by the current window is invalid. When review finds a failure, return to the earliest responsible layer: script, visual route, sequence layout, spatial contract, model pack, prop spec, source asset/facts, motion contract, integration, animation, audio, caption, or encode.

## Ending gate

Before final assembly, confirm that the ending contains:

1. a visible consequence of the climax;
2. source/cultural closure when the project promises it;
3. a concise, transferable lesson in narration and/or a readable final card.

An origin note, bibliography, or “the end” card alone does not complete an educational story. Put interaction prompts in the social package after the film has stated its own conclusion.

For a save-oriented social episode, also confirm that the declared `save_object` is present, useful without the CTA, readable at feed size, held long enough for its real word count, and consistent with the story’s earned conclusion.

## Handoff

Report project path, preview URL, master path, watermarked master path, social path, dimensions, fps, duration, codecs, audio mode, expected/confirmed voice-line count, watermark text/settings, file sizes, compression ratio, VMAF, and any intentional limitations. Include title, description, and pinned-comment options when the destination is a social platform. A moving watermark raises removal cost but cannot prevent manual reconstruction.
